# Product and marketplace notes

Families coordinating informal care need a shared picture of what was done and what the next caregiver needs to do. The first product journey starts with a recipient profile, adds a scheduled task, records the outcome and saves a handover for acknowledgement. The strongest proposed differentiation is a reviewed, timestamped handover that works locally on a shared tablet. Demand for this workflow is a product hypothesis.

[Caring Village](https://caringvillage.com/app/) offers shared calendars, medication management, care plans and journal entries. The [Alzheimer’s Association care team guide](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/care-team-calendar) identifies tools such as Lotsa Helping Hands and Caring Village for coordinating helpers. Carehando’s provisional name still requires trademark and marketplace clearance. Store keyword proposals reflect the jobs the app supports. No measured search volumes are available.

## Distribution

Amazon still distributes Fire tablet applications. It ended Amazon Appstore support for general Android phones and tablets on 20 August 2025. Android compatibility testing can still be performed for the same native code. A separate non-Fire store launch would need its own channel and billing scope. [Amazon announcement](https://developer.amazon.com/apps-and-games/blogs/2025/02/upcoming-changes-to-amazon-appstore-for-android-devices-and-coins-program)

The suggested one-time Premium test price is £4.99. Enter and review the price in the Developer Console when configuring the SKU. The client displays the store-provided localised price. Keep pricing out of metadata. Consider Lifestyle or Productivity based on available Developer Console categories and the product’s planning focus. [Amazon listing policy](https://developer.amazon.com/docs/policy-center/listing-promo.html)

## Locale and market mapping

- App interface catalogues cover en-GB, en-US, en-AU, es-ES and fr-FR. The English variants share one catalogue and format dates and times using the selected locale. The 14 remaining requested interface catalogues need translation and review.
- Listing drafts are available for UK, US and Australian English, French and Spanish. Confirm listing tabs in the console. Amazon distinguishes generic Spanish from Spanish (Mexico). French listing assets do not confer a separate Canadian French in-app translation.
- Candidate initial territories are the UK, US, Australia, France and Spain. Final availability depends on device filtering, commercial approval and data protection review. Amazon publishes country-to-marketplace mapping separately from binary languages. [Amazon international distribution](https://www.developer.amazon.com/docs/app-submission/distribute-your-app-internationally.html)
- Speech recognition and synthesis are each tested separately per device and locale. UI translation and a valid command dictionary do not establish speech-engine compatibility.
