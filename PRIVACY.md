# Carehando privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026. Carehando is published by Lanray. For privacy questions or requests, email [banksmi@mail.com](mailto:banksmi@mail.com).

## Care records

Carehando works without a Carehando account. It stores the profiles, caregiver contact details, care notes, medication reminders, appointments, handovers and attachments you enter in its private storage on your device. Carehando’s servers do not receive these records. Anyone who can open an unlocked installation may be able to view them. You can enable Carehando’s optional device lock in Settings on a compatible device.

If you choose to share or export a report, attachment or backup, the selected destination receives that file. Carehando encrypts backup archives with a passphrase you choose and does not retain that passphrase. Recipients and storage providers handle files you send according to their own policies. Carehando has no advertising or analytics features.

## Voice and purchases

If you choose voice input and grant microphone access, Carehando uses your device’s speech recognition service. Depending on your device and settings, that service may process audio online. Carehando does not retain the recording. Text input is available without microphone access. Your device’s speech service also handles optional spoken feedback.

Amazon handles Appstore purchases and payment details. To verify or restore Premium, Carehando sends an Amazon user identifier and a purchase receipt identifier over HTTPS to its Cloudflare-hosted verifier. The verifier forwards them to Amazon to check whether Premium is active. The verifier does not store a purchase history. Cloudflare processes connection information to deliver and protect the verification endpoint. Carehando stores the verified entitlement and receipt identifier on the device so Premium can remain available during a temporary connection failure. Amazon handles its own account and purchase records under the Amazon privacy notice.

## Deletion and control

Use **Settings → Privacy and exports → Delete all care data** to remove Carehando care records and settings from the device. This action preserves purchase entitlement information. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes its local copy of that information. Manage external exports and backups in the apps or storage locations where you saved them. Contact Amazon about Amazon account or purchase records. For questions about data processed by Carehando, use the privacy contact above.
